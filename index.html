Guidelines

What makes a strong entry

A good name is easy to say, easy to remember, and true to what our school stands for. Keep these in mind before you submit.

01

Original & unclaimed

The name should not belong to an existing school or registered institution nearby.

02

Meaningful

Tell us why this name fits — a story, a value, or a vision it reflects.

03

Easy to carry

Simple to pronounce, translate, and print on a uniform badge or gate sign.

04

One entry each

Each participant may submit a single name. Duplicate entries are not counted.

05

Appropriate tone

Names should be respectful and suitable for an educational institution.

06

Committee's decision is final

The naming committee will shortlist and select the winning entry.
